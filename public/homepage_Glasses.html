Welcome to Riviera Opticare!



Expert Optometry in Mesa and Scottsdale, AZ

At Riviera Opticare in Mesa and Scottsdale, AZ, we provide extraordinary optometry services and vision care products for anyone who needs general eye care, contact lenses, prescription glasses, sunglasses, and comprehensive eye exams.


Our all women optometrist team in either location can assist clients with handling eyesight deficiencies and enrich their natural elegance with our wide selection of unique eyeglasses and eyewear. Whether you are seeking eye care, eyewear, or both, our professional team knows to assist you in making the choices that will best suit your lifestyle.

What Makes Us Stand Out?

At Riviera, we take pride in our setting to make clients feel relaxed upon entering. We listen keenly to our patients and their desires before providing our professional analysis. We understand that every patient is unique and we tailor our services to meet the needs of everyone who comes through our doors.

We take our time with each patient that comes through the door. Cutting corners is against our policy, so customers are guaranteed overall excellent products and service at every visit.

Scottsdale and Mesa Eyecare Is Our Priority

Our objective is to surpass your expectations and provide only the highest customer service to make you satisfied and maintain your eye's health. If you have dry eye issues, our optometrist is ready to make a diagnosis once you have made an appointment.

We only suggest services and products that are in your best interest. We try to understand your lifestyle, whether you do outdoor sports, read a lot, work at night, etc., so that you can get eyewear that is suitable for your activities and environment.

Quality Products

We care about your eyes and maintaining your natural beauty, which is why we offer designer frames to enhance your look. Our unique eyewear comes from renowned brands, and their various designs meet each of our client's budgets. We want you to see correctly and look good while doing it. Some of the services we offer include:

•             Comprehensive and general eye exam services

•             The most current contact lens technology for optimal vision and satisfaction (e.g., Scleral Lenses)

•             Orthokeratology, also known as Ortho-K

•             Dry Eye Treatment

•             Digital retinal imaging

•             Keratoconus Treatment

•             Bifocal Contact Lenses

These are just some of the myriad of services we deliver to meet your eye care needs.

Make Your Eyecare Appointment Today!

Are you looking for an optometrist near you or a regular eye doctor for your optometry care needs? Great eye care begins with setting up the first appointment. Contact us or call us at (480) 991-6432 in Scottsdale or (480) 827-9184 in Mesa to schedule an appointment today.

Meet Our All-Women Eye Doctor Team!

Learn Who We Are

  • Dr. Susan L. Reckell

    Dr. Susan Reckell is the Founder and President of Riviera Opticare. She attended University of Michigan for undergraduate studies and Michigan College of Optometry for her Doctorate degree. Her favorite area of Optometry and expertise is in specialty bifocal contact lenses, Keratoconus treatment contact lenses, and Scleral lenses for corneal transplants or cornea disorders. She is a member of the American Optometric Association (AOA) and the Arizona Optometric Association (AZOA).

    Read More
  • Dr. Amy Keding

    Dr. Amy Keding is originally from Northbrook, Illinois. She completed her Bachelor's Degree in Chemistry at the University of New Hampshire while also playing on the collegiate volleyball team. She then went on to receive her Doctor of Optometry Degree from the Illinois College of Optometry, graduating magna cum laude. She recently worked for a prominent eye institute in the Valley, including an additional year of training in Ocular Disease Management as well as Cataract, Corneal and LASIK surgery Post-Operative Care and Management. She is primarily focused on practicing medical optometry, though also enjoys comprehensive eye examinations and contact lens fittings for all ages.

  • Dr. Brittany Warner

    Dr. Brittany Warner is originally from Alberta Canada. She completed her undergraduate degree in Bachelor of Science with specialization in psychology at the University of Alberta. The doctor of optometry program at Midwestern University Arizona College of Optometry is what brought Dr. Warner down to Arizona. She graduated as the valedictorian and received the Dean of Excellence Award. Immediately afterwards, Dr. Warner completed a year long Ocular Disease Residency in Bakersfield California, specializing in the treatment of dry eye.

    Outside of work Dr. Warner enjoys volunteering, traveling, gardening, playing the piano, and spending quality time with her husband and three beautiful huskies.
