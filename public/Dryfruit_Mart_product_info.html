Pistachio (Piata)

Pistachios, also known as pista, are nutrient-rich dry fruits packed with protein, healthy fats, and fiber. They are an excellent source of antioxidants, which help fight inflammation and promote heart health. Regular consumption may improve cholesterol levels and support healthy blood sugar control. Pistachios also aid in weight management due to their satiating properties. Additionally, they support eye health due to their high levels of vitamin A and lutein.

Almonds (Badam)

Almonds are nutrient-dense dry fruits rich in healthy monounsaturated fats, protein, and fiber. They are an excellent source of vitamin E, which promotes skin health and acts as a powerful antioxidant. Regular almond consumption can help improve heart health by lowering bad cholesterol levels. Almonds are also beneficial for weight management, as they help keep you feeling full longer. Additionally, they support brain function and memory due to their high content of vitamin B2 and magnesium.

Cashew (Kaju)

Cashews are a rich source of healthy fats, protein, and essential minerals like magnesium, zinc, and iron. They help support heart health by improving cholesterol levels and reducing the risk of cardiovascular diseases. The antioxidants in cashews protect the body from oxidative stress and inflammation. Regular consumption can improve bone health due to their high magnesium content. Cashews also promote eye health, as they contain lutein and zeaxanthin, which are beneficial for vision.

Raisin (Kismis)

These dried fruits, available in golden, black-ish, and green hues, are nutritional powerhouses. Packed with fiber, iron, calcium, potassium, and various essential nutrients, they offer an energy boost and contribute to the health of hair and skin.Raisins also contain natural compounds that stimulate the production of digestive enzymes, facilitating the breakdown of food. Additionally, they possess mild laxative properties that can help alleviate digestive discomfort. Including raisins in your diet can contribute to improved digestion and overall gastrointestinal health.
